# Gerber E-Myth Systems

Distilled from Michael Gerber's *The E-Myth Revisited*: small businesses
fail because technicians (doers) found companies needing entrepreneurs
(visionaries) and managers (systematizers) too. The fix: build a turn-key
franchise prototype — a business that runs on systems, not on you.

## Purpose

Free the owner from operations by building the business as a replicable
system of documented roles and processes — sellable, delegable, survivable.

## The three personalities (staff the business with all three)

- **Entrepreneur:** future, vision, change. Asks "what could be?"
- **Manager:** order, systems, predictability. Asks "how do we run it?"
- **Technician:** present work, craft. Asks "how do I do it?"
Most owners are 10/20/70 and drown doing. Rebalance deliberately: scheduled
entrepreneur time (strategy, weekly), manager rituals (systems review),
technician hours capped (or the business owns you, not vice versa).

## The franchise prototype method

1. **Model it as if franchising to 1000.** Every role documented to the
   standard a stranger could operate (position contracts: results expected,
   standards, signature). If it only works with YOU in it, it is a job with
   overhead, not a business.
2. **Systems before people.** Hire to the system (clear role, trained in
   days from docs), never build around irreplaceable heroes. Org chart by
   FUNCTION with one accountable owner per box — org charts are accountability
   maps, not hierarchies for ego.
3. **Quantify everything customers feel.** Standards stated as numbers
   (answer in 3 rings, delivery in 48h, defect rate): unmeasured standards
   are wishes. Mystery-shop your own business quarterly.
4. **Innovation, quantification, orchestration (the loop).** Try (bounded
   experiments in ONE unit), measure (did the number move?), roll out
   winners system-wide via updated docs. Continuous, scheduled, budgeted —
   not "when we have time".

## Verification

Owner-independence test: two unplugged weeks with phone off — revenue,
quality, and morale steady means systems work; any fire that needed YOU is
named, systematized, and retested next quarter. Freedom is measured, not
felt.
