# Iansiti AI Factory

Distilled from Iansiti & Lakhani *Competing in the Age of AI*: digital
firms win by rebuilding around the AI factory — data pipelines feeding
algorithms that decide, learn, and improve with scale. Transformation means
changing the operating model, not buying tools.

## Purpose

Turn AI from pilots into the firm's decision engine: instrumented
operations, algorithmic decisions at scale, and compounding data advantage.

## The factory (four connected loops)

1. **Data pipeline.** Every operation emits structured, labeled data by
   design (instrumentation is architecture, not logging). Pipelines versioned
   and governed; data quality owned like product quality — garbage data
   compounds AGAINST you at AI scale.
2. **Algorithmic decisions.** Move decisions from meetings to models where
   volume/velocity demand it (pricing, routing, matching, moderation):
   decision inventory first (what gets decided, at what rate, current accuracy),
   automate the high-volume decidable ones, keep humans on the ambiguous
   tail with model assistance.
3. **Experimentation platform.** Every change is a testable hypothesis with
   randomized rollout, guardrail metrics, and kill criteria (the discipline
   from lean/agile, industrialized). Experiment velocity IS the learning
   rate of the firm — measure and raise it.
4. **Software foundation.** API-first everything (internal products as
   composable services), cloud scale, modular architecture that lets teams
   deploy the factory's pieces independently. Legacy wrapped and strangled,
   never big-banged.

## Collision management (why transformations stall)

- Operating-model clash: factory (experiments, data, algorithms) vs legacy
  (hierarchy, opinion, process) — run both explicitly with a migration map,
  or the legacy model eats the pilots.
- Ethics/scale debt: biased data and unchecked automation compound harm at
  factory speed — governance (bias audits, human override paths) ships WITH
  the factory, not after the incident.
- Talent: analysts who code, engineers who model, managers who experiment —
  hiring and training follow the model, not the old org chart.

## Verification

Transformation review: decision inventory with automation share trending up,
experiment velocity tracked, data-quality SLAs green, one legacy process
strangled per quarter. AI budgets without these metrics are IT spending with
better branding.
