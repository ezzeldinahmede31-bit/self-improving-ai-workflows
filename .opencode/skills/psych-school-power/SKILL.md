## Clinic rules (defensive ethic)

1. Maneuver against COMPETITORS and OBJECTIONS — never deceive patients or owners.
2. Every tactic must survive daylight: if exposed, it still looks fair.
3. No fake reviews, no fake scarcity, no sabotage — power without honor backfires in tribes.
4. Win the frame (missed-call math), then the fight is over before it starts.

## Verification

Every competitive move must cite its law/source + pass the daylight test. Moves
that need secrecy to look fair = rejected.
