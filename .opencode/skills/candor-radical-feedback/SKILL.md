# Candor Radical Feedback

Distilled from Kim Scott's *Radical Candor*: the best feedback is Care
Personally + Challenge Directly. Everything else is a failure mode with a
name — learn the names to escape them fast.

## Purpose

Build a team where truth flows in all directions: guidance given, received,
and demanded as the normal operating state — not a dreaded event.

## The compass (diagnose yourself first)

- **Radical Candor** (care + challenge): the goal. Humble, helpful, immediate,
  in private for criticism.
- **Ruinous Empathy** (care, no challenge): praise-only niceness that lets
  people fail slowly. The most common failure in kind teams.
- **Manipulative Insincerity** (neither): empty praise and political
  back-channeling. Poison — exit people who live here.
- **Obnoxious Aggression** (challenge, no care): brutal honesty that bruises.
  Better than silence, worse than candor; fixable if the care is real.

## The practices

1. **Ask first, then give.** Solicit criticism before offering it ("what
   could I do better?") — leaders model receiving before demanding giving.
   Praise in public AND private; criticize in private, quickly (days, not
   quarters).
2. **Be specific and kind, not nice.** Situation-behavior-impact, not traits:
   "in yesterday's review you dismissed two questions (behavior) which shut
   down the junior's input (impact)". Kind = honest about what helps them;
   nice = comfortable for you.
3. **Praise to criticize ratio is a myth; specificity is the point.** Vague
   praise ("great job") teaches nothing — name what was great and why so it
   repeats. Same for criticism: the fix must be visible inside the feedback.
4. **Career conversations (the care half).** Know each person's growth
   trajectory (superstar vs rockstar modes are BOTH honorable); feedback
   lands when people believe you want their success, proven in 1:1s about
   THEIR goals, not your tasks.
5. **Gauge, don't guess.** Ask "was that helpful?" after hard feedback;
   calibrate per person (some need bluntness, some need cushioning — same
   honesty, different delivery). Feedback about feedback closes the loop.

## Verification

Monthly: each report got specific praise AND specific criticism (private
log check); solicited criticism received and acted on visibly; no feedback
older than two weeks unspoken. Silence is data — about fear, not harmony.
