# Schwartz Breakthrough Advertising

Distilled from Eugene Schwartz's *Breakthrough Advertising*: you cannot
CREATE desire — you can only channel existing mass desire (spread across
millions) into your product. Copy fails when it speaks to the wrong stage
of awareness or the wrong stage of sophistication.

## Purpose

Match every message to the prospect's awareness stage and the market's
sophistication stage — the two coordinates that decide what can be said.

## The coordinates (diagnose before writing)

1. **Five stages of awareness.** Unaware (no knowledge of problem/product) →
   Problem-aware → Solution-aware → Product-aware → Most-aware (knows you,
   just needs the deal). Rule: never sell the PRODUCT to stages 1-2 (sell
   the problem/story), never educate stage 5 (give the offer). Most failed
   copy is stage-mismatched, not badly written.
2. **Five stages of sophistication.** How saturated is the market with similar promises?
   Stage 1: state the claim. Stage 2: enlarge it. Stage 3:
   introduce the MECHANISM (how it works — the differentiator). Stage 4:
   enlarge the mechanism. Stage 5: identity/experience (claims exhausted).
   Mechanism beats adjectives in stages 3+ — novelty of HOW wins.
3. **Mass desire + states of mind.** Find the pre-existing desire (health,
   status, belonging, relief...) and attach the product as its fulfillment
   vehicle. Intensify (stakes, vividness), identify (the prospect sees
   themselves), graduate the claim believability (each promise only one step
   beyond what they already accept).

## Headline architecture (Schwartz-tested)

- Lead with the awareness-appropriate promise (problem agitation early,
  mechanism mid, offer late). Specificity + news + curiosity-with-payoff.
  Long headlines outsell short when they carry the mechanism.
- Body expands the mechanism: proof stacking (demonstration, data,
  testimony in that persuasiveness order), objection demolition in the
  prospect's own words, intensification toward the close.

## Verification

Copy review: awareness stage named with evidence, sophistication stage named
with competitor-claim history, mechanism stated in one sentence, mass desire
named, believability steps checked. Stage mismatch = rewrite, not polish.
