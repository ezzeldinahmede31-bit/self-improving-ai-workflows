# Enterprise Agile Transformation (David J. Anderson et al.) / Kanban discipline

Kanban-based transformation changes the process by changing the system of work: visualize, limit, and improve. This skill applies that discipline to teams and organizations.

## Start where you are

- Change the current process from where it stands; the first step is to see it clearly.
- Respect the existing roles and responsibilities; evolution, not revolution.
- The process change is safest when it is a small, agreed experiment.

## Visualize and limit WIP

- A board makes the work and its flow visible; the columns are the stages of the value stream.
- Work in progress limits expose the bottleneck and stop overloading.
- The system pulls work only when capacity is free; the pull is the discipline.

## Manage flow and policies

- Flow is measured by lead time and throughput; the metrics are the steering wheel.
- Make the policies explicit: definitions of done, priority rules, and escalation paths.
- The policies are tested and improved by the team, not imposed from outside.

## Improve collaboratively

- The process improves through experiments, not mandates; each change is reviewed by its effect.
- The cadence (replenishment, delivery, retro) is the rhythm of improvement.
- The system evolves continuously; the goal is a better system, not a finished state.
