# Sugarman Adweek Triggers

Distilled from Joseph Sugarman's *Advertising Secrets of the Written Word*
(Adweek handbook): every element must make reading the NEXT element
irresistible — the slippery slide — powered by psychological triggers and
stories that sell without feeling like selling.

## Purpose

Write copy readers cannot put down: momentum engineering plus the buying
triggers that convert attention into orders.

## The mechanics

1. **The slippery slide.** First sentence short (5 words or so) and
   curiosity-complete-incomplete: finished enough to satisfy, open enough to
   require the next. Every sentence's job is the next sentence; every
   paragraph's job is the next paragraph. Test by reading aloud — friction
   you hear is friction buyers feel.
2. **Story as vehicle.** Personal/experience stories carry claims past
   skepticism (the BluBlocker pattern: inventor's journey → problem →
   discovery → proof). Specifics (names, places, numbers, setbacks) make it
   believed; vagueness makes it an ad. Never break the story for the pitch —
   the pitch RIDES the story.
3. **Buying triggers (deploy deliberately).** Curiosity seeds (unanswered
   questions planted early, paid off late); specificity (exact figures beat
   round claims); honesty about flaws (disarming disclaimers raise trust in
   strengths); exclusivity and belonging; authority via demonstration (show,
   don't assert); urgency from real scarcity (manufactured urgency detected
   and punished by refund rates).
4. **Harmony of elements.** Headline, art, first line, close all sell the
   SAME idea in different registers. Dissonant cleverness (brilliant line
   serving a different idea) breaks the slide — cut it no matter how much
   you love it.
5. **Edit for momentum.** Kill throat-clearing openings; vary sentence
   length (staccato for speed, rolling for immersion); subheads as rest
   stops that re-hook; never end a page/section on a low-energy line.

## Verification

Copy review: first-sentence test passed (must read second), story specificity
scored, trigger inventory listed per section, harmony check (one idea
throughout), read-aloud friction log empty. Copy that can be put down will
be put down.
