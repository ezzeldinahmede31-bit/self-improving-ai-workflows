# Clean Agile: Back to Basics (Robert C. Martin)

Clean Agile returns agile to its essentials: small teams, short feedback, working software, and the practices that make it sustainable. This skill applies those basics.

## The values and principles

- The manifesto values individuals, working software, customer collaboration, and responding to change.
- Principles, not ceremonies, are the contract; if a ritual does not serve a principle, drop it.
- Agile is for people building software; the team and the customer are the unit.

## Roles and collaboration

- The customer decides what and prioritizes; the developer decides how and estimates.
- Collaboration replaces handoffs; the team and the customer talk daily.
- The standup and the demo are feedback loops, not report cards.

## Estimation and planning

- Estimate relative size with story points; the team calibrates over time.
- Plan iteratively: the backlog is groomed, the iteration is committed, and the demo shows reality.
- Velocity is a fact about the team, not a target; use it to forecast, not to punish.

## The engineering practices

- Test-driven development and continuous integration keep each change safe.
- Refactoring keeps the design open to the change the customer will ask for next.
- Sustainably paced teams deliver more; burnout is a planning failure.
