# Peopleware: Productive Projects and Teams (DeMarco & Lister)

Peopleware argues the biggest risks in software projects are human, not technical. This skill applies that insight to team, workspace, and management decisions.

## People over process

- The primary cost factor is the people and their interaction, not the tools or the process.
- A good team is worth more than a good process; protect the team.
- The team is a whole; its chemistry is the productivity engine.

## The workplace

- Quiet time is the most valuable resource a knowledge worker has; interruptions destroy it.
- The workspace design shapes focus: private space, controlled noise, and the right collaboration areas.
- A closed-door policy is not rudeness; it is capacity management.

## Overtime and burnout

- Overtime is a capacity debt; the short-term gain is repaid with interest.
- Sustained pace beats heroic bursts; the schedule must fit the humans.
- Burnout is a management failure; the manager owns the pace.

## Teams and management

- Team formation takes time; churn resets it.
- The manager's job is to remove obstacles and provide context, not to inspect work.
- The sociology of the project decides the outcome; manage it deliberately.
