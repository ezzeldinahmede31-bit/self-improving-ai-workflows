# Ogilvy on Advertising

Distilled from David Ogilvy's *Ogilvy on Advertising* + *Confessions*:
the consumer is not a moron, she is your wife — respect her intelligence,
research her desires, and sell with information that keeps selling for years.

## Purpose

Create advertising that builds brands AND moves product: researched,
headline-led, product-honest, and consistent enough to compound.

## The doctrines (non-negotiable)

1. **Research first, always.** Study the product, the consumer, the
   competition, and past advertising in the category before writing a word.
   The best ideas come from finding the true, remarkable fact competitors
   cannot claim — then saying it plainly.
2. **Headlines carry 80% of the money.** The headline is read 5x more than
   body copy: promise a benefit (or news, or curiosity with payoff), name
   the audience when relevant, keep it specific (numbers beat adjectives).
   Test headlines first; everything else is optimization of a good headline.
3. **Long copy sells (when it informs).** Facts, specifics, and reasons-why
   outperform slogans for anything considered before purchase. Write all the
   copy the prospect needs — then cut every word they don't.
4. **Brand image compounds.** Every ad is a long-term investment in what the
   brand MEANS. Consistency of personality across years beats clever
   one-offs that win awards and lose sales. Never chase novelty that
   contradicts the image.
5. **Position honestly.** Find what the product IS in the consumer's mind
   and own it (the positioning insight later formalized by Ries & Trout).
   Superiority claims need proof; puffery ("best", "leading") is wallpaper.
6. **Direct response discipline everywhere.** Countable results (coupons,
   codes, tracked URLs then; pixels and attribution now): advertising that
   cannot show its sales is entertainment with an invoice.

## Verification

Campaign review: research file attached, headline options ranked with
reasons, body copy fact-density checked, brand-image consistency judged
against past work, measurement plan defined. Clever without selling is
rejected — awards don't pay.
