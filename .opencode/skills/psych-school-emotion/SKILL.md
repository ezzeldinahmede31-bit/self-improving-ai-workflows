# Emotion School

## Purpose

Distill drives-and-habits science (verified ranks in
`memory/psychology-books-index.json`) into motivation rules. Single purpose:
DRIVE — what moves owners to act and stay.

## Clinic rules

1. Every video runs anxiety → relief, never fear without rescue.
2. Sell one daily habit per feature (agenda at 8am, confirmations at noon).
3. Recall/reactivation content uses care language, not sales language.
4. Retention metric: logins per week per clinic — falling logins precede churn.

## Verification

Every retention/recall piece must name its drive (fear/status/belonging/habit)
and its habit loop (cue-routine-reward). Missing loop = rewrite.
