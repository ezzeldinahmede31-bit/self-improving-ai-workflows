# Sheridan They Ask You Answer

Distilled from Marcus Sheridan's *They Ask, You Answer*: buyers complete
most of the journey before contacting sales — the company that answers
their questions best (especially pricing, problems, comparisons) wins the
trust and the deal.

## Purpose

Make content the sales team: every buyer question answered honestly on your
site, so prospects arrive pre-sold and sales cycles collapse.

## The playbook (in adoption order)

1. **Mine the questions.** Sales + support call logs, emails, chat
   transcripts → every question buyers actually ask, ranked by frequency.
   The list IS the editorial calendar for a year — no brainstorming needed.
2. **Answer the scary five first.** Cost/pricing, problems/risks,
   comparisons (you vs named competitors), reviews (including bad ones),
   best-in-class lists (even naming competitors). Companies dodge these;
   answering them is the differentiation. Evasion detected = trust lost.
3. **Teach, don't sell, in the content.** Educator tone (help them decide
   rightly even if elsewhere), visible authorship (real experts sign
   pieces), updated dates shown (stale answers erode trust silently).
4. **Assignment selling.** Sales assigns content before calls ("watch this
   4-min video, read this comparison, then let's talk") — prospects arrive
   educated, calls convert at multiples. Track assignment → close-rate lift
   as THE metric.
5. **Video for the human half.** On-camera experts answering top questions
   (imperfect beats polished — trust over production value); every video
   transcribed into text (search + skimmers), embedded beside its article.

## Verification

Program review: question bank exists with frequency ranks, scary-five
coverage complete (or scheduled with dates), assignment-selling active with
lift measured, content freshness audited quarterly. Content without buyer
questions behind it is publishing, not selling.
