# Made to Stick (Heath)

Ideas stick when they follow SUCCESs.

## Workflow
1. Simplify to core; strip non-essential.
2. Add unexpected hook to earn attention.
3. Make concrete with examples, not abstractions.
4. Add credibility + emotion + story.

## Core Rules
- Curse of knowledge is the enemy.
