# Caples Tested Advertising Methods

Distilled from John Caples' *Tested Advertising Methods*: a lifetime of
split-run tests compressed into headline formulas and curiosity mechanics —
what consistently wins, measured in orders, not opinions.

## Purpose

Write headlines and leads that pull: benefit-first formulas, curiosity that
pays off, and continuous testing as the operating system.

## The formulas (each earned by testing)

1. **Benefit headlines first.** Start with "Get", "How to", "Why", numbers,
   and the reader's self-interest ("They Laughed When I Sat Down at the
   Piano — But When I Started to Play!"). The best headlines promise a
   reward for reading on AND deliver it in the body — curiosity without
   payoff is a trick that burns trust permanently.
2. **The headline checklist.** Does it: offer a benefit or news? Name the
   audience (or repel non-buyers)? Arouse curiosity with a credible promise
   of satisfaction? Stay believable (specifics > superlatives)? Read
   effortlessly (short words, one idea)? Five yeses or rewrite.
3. **Lead types that work.** News lead (announce), story lead (dramatize a
   use case), benefit-summary lead (compress the offer), question lead
   (only with an answer the copy gives). Match the lead to the awareness
   stage (Schwartz coordinates apply).
4. **Body disciplines.** First paragraph continues the headline's promise
   (never a new topic); subheads carry skimmers (each subhead a mini-
   headline); close with explicit action + reason to act now (offer,
   guarantee, deadline — honest ones only).
5. **Test matrix, always on.** Headlines × offers × layouts in rotation;
   winners challenged by new variants (champions never retire undefeated —
   they get beaten eventually). Records kept: what won, by how much, why
   you think so (the log compounds into institutional knowledge).

## Verification

Copy review: formula named per headline, checklist five-yeses, lead type
matched to awareness, payoff present for every curiosity opener, test plan
attached. Untested cleverness ships as a test cell, never as the control.
