# Whitman Cashvertising

Distilled from Drew Whitman's *Cashvertising*: buying is emotional first —
eight biological Life-Force desires plus nine learned wants drive every
purchase; 41 techniques map them onto copy. Diagnose the desire, deploy the
techniques.

## Purpose

Aim every message at a primal buying desire with the technique that
activates it — no generic persuasion, only targeted triggers.

## The Life-Force 8 (biological, strongest first)

1. Survival, enjoyment of life, life extension. 2. Enjoyment of food and
   beverages. 3. Freedom from fear, pain, and danger. 4. Sexual
   companionship. 5. Comfortable living conditions. 6. To be superior, win,
   keep up. 7. Care and protection of loved ones. 8. Social approval.
Plus nine learned wants: wealth, beauty, health (as project), popularity,
and five more — secondary but stackable.

## Technique deployment (the high-hit-rate subset)

- **Specificity + proof stacking:** exact numbers, demonstrations, before/
  after, testimonials in the prospect's own words (third-party proof beats
  claims 10:1).
- **Fear/agitation done ethically:** name the real cost of inaction (with
  evidence), then position relief — never manufactured dread.
- **Greed framed as smart:** discounts as intelligence ("smart buyers..."),
  bonuses stacking value past price, payment plans shrinking pain.
- **Authority transfer:** expert endorsement, credentials shown (not told),
  process transparency (how-it's-made builds trust faster than claims).
- **Convenience as benefit:** every friction removed stated explicitly
  (done-for-you, one-click, pre-filled) — laziness is a buying motive, honor
  it.
- **Curiosity gaps with payoff** (Caples discipline applies), **exclusivity**
  (real limits), **belonging** (identity of owners, not buyers).

## Verification

Trigger audit per piece: Life-Force desire named (1-8 + learned wants),
techniques listed per section, proof-to-claim ratio checked (claims without
adjacent proof flagged), fear/urgency claims evidenced. Untriggered copy is
information, not advertising.
