# Shape Up Basecamp

Distilled from Ryan Singer's *Shape Up*: stop managing backlogs — shape work
to fit fixed appetites, bet on the best shapes, build uninterrupted, cool
down. Six weeks max, or the work is wrongly shaped.

## Purpose

Convert vague ideas into shippable, de-risked projects with fixed time and
variable scope — ending chronic overrun without micromanagement.

## The cycle

1. **Shape (before betting).** A shaper roughs out the idea: problem
   statement, appetite (how much time is this WORTH — 2 or 6 weeks, decided
   up front), solution sketch at the right altitude (concrete enough to
   evaluate, abstract enough to leave design freedom), rabbit holes flagged
   (named unknowns with proposed handling), no-gos (explicit out-of-scope).
   Unshaped work never reaches betting — shaping IS the intake gate.
2. **Bet (the betting table).** Decision-makers bet appetites on shaped
   pitches, compare against each other and against doing nothing. Bets are
   commitments: the team gets the full cycle uninterrupted. No mid-cycle
   reshuffling — the table meets again next cycle.
3. **Build (uninterrupted).** Small team (1-3) owns the whole project. Scope
   hammering as reality hits: cut scope (never quality) to fit the appetite
   — must-haves shrink, nice-to-haves fall to a documented pile. Integrator
   mindset: daily visible progress in the real product, not branches and
   status decks.
4. **Cool-down (2 weeks).** No scheduled project work: bug fixes, exploration,
   tech health, and shaping the NEXT cycle's pitches. Cool-down is load-
   bearing — skipping it borrows morale from the future at loan-shark rates.

## Rules that make it work

- Appetite before solution (time is the constraint, scope the variable —
   the inverse of death-march planning). Circuit breaker: projects that miss
   appetite get re-shaped and re-bet, never extended mid-flight.
- Hill charts for honest status (uphill = figuring out, downhill =
   executing) instead of percentage theater.

## Verification

Pitch packet per bet: problem, appetite, sketch, rabbit holes, no-gos.
Cycle review: shipped within appetite (scope-hammered visibly), cool-down
protected, missed bets re-shaped not extended. Backlog size is not a metric.
