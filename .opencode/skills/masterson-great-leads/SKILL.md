# Masterson Great Leads

Distilled from Mark Ford (Masterson) & John Forde's *Great Leads*: the lead
(first 10-20% of copy) does 80% of the selling — six lead types, each matched
to awareness and desire. Wrong lead type, lost sale, whatever follows.

## Purpose

Open every promotion with the lead type its market demands — matched to
awareness, desire, and sophistication.

## The six leads (diagnose, then deploy)

1. **Offer lead.** Price/deal/terms up front — for the most-aware buyers who
   comparison-shop. Requirements: genuinely superior deal + proof it is one.
   Weak offers exposed instantly here; strengthen the offer, not the words.
2. **Promise lead.** Big specific transformation promised, mechanism hinted —
   for problem/solution-aware markets with trust. The promise must be
   believable (one step past accepted) AND provable later in the copy.
3. **Problem-solution lead.** Agitate the painful problem, position the
   product as the resolution path — for problem-aware markets feeling the
   pain now. Pain specificity (their words, their scenes) decides power.
4. **Secret lead.** The unknown mechanism/fact insiders know ("the
   little-known...") — for sophisticated markets bored of promises.
   Requires a REAL secret (novel mechanism, hidden cause); fake secrets
   detected and punished by refund rates.
5. **Story lead.** Dramatic narrative carrying the selling idea — for
   unaware/early markets needing emotional entry. Story MUST converge on
   the mechanism (entertainment that never lands the pitch is expensive
   theater).
6. **Proclamation/Invitation lead.** Direct announcement or exclusive
   invitation — for lists, launches, and events where relationship precedes
   persuasion. Exclusivity must be real (segment-gated, capped, or timed).

## Verification

Lead review: type named, awareness-match argued, promise believability
checked against sophistication, payoff present (curiosity leads), offer
strength judged for offer leads. Mismatched lead = rewrite the opening,
not the close.
