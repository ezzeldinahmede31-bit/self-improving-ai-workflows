# Vaynerchuk Jab Jab Hook

Distilled from Gary Vaynerchuk's *Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook*: social is a
cocktail party, not a billboard — give (jabs: entertain/inform/inspire, NATIVE
to each platform) repeatedly, then earn the right to ask (the hook).

## Purpose

Turn social channels from broadcast pipes into communities that convert —
by matching content to platform culture and asking rarely but effectively.

## The discipline

1. **Respect the platform's native language.** Images tell on Instagram,
   conversation on X, professionalism on LinkedIn, entertainment on TikTok,
   community on Facebook. Cross-posting the same asset everywhere is telling
   a joke in the wrong language — re-craft per platform, same idea.
2. **Jabs: entertain, inform, inspire.** The daily work: micro-content that
   gives (laughs, tips, behind-scenes, recognition of followers). Jabs build
   the equity the hook spends. Ratio discipline: many gives per ask —
   accounts that only hook get muted.
3. **Hooks done right.** The ask is explicit, easy, and timed (after equity):
   one action, frictionless path, real reason-now. Every hook names what the
   follower gets for acting (not what YOU get).
4. **Storytelling over slogans.** Document, don't create: process, customers,
   failures, lessons — real beats polished. Characters (people, not logos)
   carry series; consistency of voice across chaos of volume.
5. **Listen and reply at scale.** Comments are the cocktail conversation:
   answer fast, with personality, especially critics (public recovery converts
   observers). Social listening feeds the content calendar with what the
   room actually discusses.

## Verification

Account audit: platform-native ratio (re-crafted vs cross-posted), jab:hook
ratio over 30 days, reply rate + median reply time, hook conversion per ask.
Broadcast-only accounts get the prescription: 30 days of jabs first.
