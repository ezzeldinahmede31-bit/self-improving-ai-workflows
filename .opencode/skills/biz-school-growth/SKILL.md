## Clinic rules

1. Cadence compounds: 4-5 videos/week beats 20 videos once.
2. One metric per loop (views→WhatsApp→demos→pilots); fix the worst conversion first.
3. Document every win as a reusable template (agency asset, not effort).
4. Protect maker time: batch content, automate distribution, review weekly.

## Verification

Every growth plan must state: cadence, one metric, template produced, review
day — each with its canon source. Vanity metrics = rejected.
