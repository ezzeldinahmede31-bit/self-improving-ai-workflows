# Introduction to Probability

## When to use
Use when a probabilistic calculation must be done correctly, or when the user asks about conditional probability, expectation, or the central limit theorem.

## Core mechanics
- Apply the axioms of probability and counting methods.
- Use conditional probability and Bayes' rule.
- Define random variables and their expectations.
- Use the law of large numbers and the central limit theorem.
- Reason about dependence and independence.
- Validate with simulations.

## Verification
- Verify a calculation by simulation.
- Check a distribution against a closed form.
- Test the central limit theorem on a sample of sums.
