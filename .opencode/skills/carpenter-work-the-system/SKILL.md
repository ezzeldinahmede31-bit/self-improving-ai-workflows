# Carpenter Work the System

Distilled from Sam Carpenter's *Work the System*: businesses are collections
of systems (linear processes with inputs/outputs) — document them, separate
yourself from them, optimize them one at a time, and the business runs
without heroics.

## Purpose

Convert tribal-knowledge operations into documented, improvable systems so
the business performs consistently regardless of who is in the room.

## The method (in order — sequence matters)

1. **Strategic objective (one page).** What the business IS in plain terms:
   product/service, customer, scale, and the owner's role target (working ON
   vs IN hours per week). Every system decision references this page.
2. **Document every system.** Each recurring process gets: name, purpose,
   trigger, steps in order, owner, inputs/outputs, and quality check. Start
   with the money-critical and chaos-prone (sales, fulfillment, support);
   perfection later, coverage now. Undocumented = nonexistent.
3. **Separate and observe.** Step back from execution into the observer role
   (even partially): run the business from the documentation for a week and
   log every deviation — deviations ARE the improvement backlog, gifted by
   reality.
4. **Optimize one system at a time.** Pick the highest-pain system, fix its
   root cause (usually missing steps, unclear ownership, or no quality
   check), update the document, move on. Small sequential wins compound;
   parallel overhauls collide.
5. **Maintain mechanically.** Scheduled document reviews (quarterly per
   system), change log per system, new-hire onboarding = reading + shadowing
   the docs. Systems rot without maintenance schedules — calendar them.

## The mindset shifts (the actual product)

- From firefighter to mechanic: recurring fires are missing systems, not
  bad luck. From indispensable to replaceable (including yourself): your
  value moves up the stack as systems absorb your old job. From people-
  blame to system-blame: 95% of errors are system design, fix the system.

## Verification

Quarterly: strategic objective current, system inventory complete for
revenue-critical paths, deviation log shrinking, each system with owner +
review date. Fires recurring without new documentation are management
failures, logged as such.
