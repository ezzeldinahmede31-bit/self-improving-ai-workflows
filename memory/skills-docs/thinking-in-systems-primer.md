## What it does

Applies Donella Meadows' Thinking in Systems to analyze any complex system — software, business, infrastructure, automation — through its structure rather than its events: stocks and flows, feedback loops (balancing and reinforcing), delays, system archetypes (shifting the burden, tragedy of the commons, fixes that fail, growth and underinvestment), leverage points (where to intervene with the greatest effect for the least effort), and the traps that follow from ignoring structure.
