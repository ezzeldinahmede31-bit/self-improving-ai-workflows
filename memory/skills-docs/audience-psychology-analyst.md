## What it does

Build a psychological profile of any target audience and translate it into marketing AND video-editing decisions. Covers psychographic segmentation (needs, motivations, fears, values, decision style), emotional drivers mapped to Maslow/Ekman, dual-process persuasion (Kahneman System 1/2), attention science (3-second hook, retention curves, cognitive load), and how to apply all of it to ad copy, offers, AND video montage (pacing, cuts, music, color, hooks, emotional arc). Use when the user says: 'analyze my audience psychologically', 'why do people buy this', 'what does my audience fear/want', 'psychology of my customers', 'make the video hold attention', 'hook psychology', 'emotional arc for my video edit', 'من هو جمهوري نفسياً', 'ايه اللي بيشتري مني وليه', 'ازاي أخلي الفيديو ممسك الانتباه'. For pure persuasion principles see influence-psychology / persuasion-principles; for scroll-stopping hooks see viral-hooks.
