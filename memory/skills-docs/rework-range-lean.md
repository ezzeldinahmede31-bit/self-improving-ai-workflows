## What it does

Applies the counter-conventional product and business principles from Rework (Fried & Hansson): small is a feature not a stage, constraints are an advantage, scratch your own itch, start a business not a startup, go to sleep and the business still runs, workaholism is not productivity, and cut the work to the essential. Covers saying no to scope, the 'less is a choice' mentality, deciding on a tiny audience, and shipping a finished small thing instead of a perpetual beta. Use when the user says 'we are too small', 'we need more people', 'how do we compete with the big ones', 'scope creep', 'we keep planning and never ship', 'Rework', '37signals', 'start smaller', 'do less', 'why do we need this many features', or when a project is bloating under its own ambition.
