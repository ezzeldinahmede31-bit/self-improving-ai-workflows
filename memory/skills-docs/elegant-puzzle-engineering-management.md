## What it does

Applies Will Larson's An Elegant Puzzle to managing engineering teams as a solvable system: treat management as a collection of puzzles (systems of goals, organizations, tools, approaches) and engineer each one deliberately. Covers team design and org shape, the systems view of management (change levers, stable vs dynamic systems), project management and roadmapping, mentoring and growth, and the day-to-day habits of effective managers (1:1s, reviews, feedback).
