## What it does

Applies James Clear's Atomic Habits to systems change: tiny 1% improvements compound; habits are built by systems, not by goals. Covers the four laws of behavior change (make it obvious, attractive, easy, satisfying) and their inverses (make it invisible, unattractive, difficult, unsatisfying), identity-based habits (become the person who does the thing), habit stacking, the 2-minute rule, and environment design so good behavior is the default path.
