## What it does

Applies Wil van der Aalst's Process Mining to extract real process behavior from event logs — discovery (automatically building the actual process model from events), conformance checking (finding where reality deviates from the intended model), and enhancement (repairing or extending the model with frequencies and times). Covers the alpha and inductive mining families, event-log quality, and the honest limits of the field.
