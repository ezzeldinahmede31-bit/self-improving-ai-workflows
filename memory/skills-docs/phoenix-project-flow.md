## What it does

Applies the Theory of Constraints from The Phoenix Project (Kim, Behr, Spafford) to IT operations and delivery: the goal is throughput of the whole value stream, not utilization of any one team; identify the single system bottleneck (the constraint), exploit it, subordinate everything else, elevate it, then recheck. Covers the Four Types of Work (business projects, IT operations, internal projects, changes), the work center, DevOps improvements as a constraint-breaker, and the improvement kata of daily standups that attack the bottleneck.
